## Aura — 60–90s hackathon pitch script

### 1) Problem
Factories are deploying more autonomous robots, but supervision and compliance are still manual, fragmented, and slow—especially when something goes abnormal.

### 2) Why current factory operations are fragmented
Telemetry lives in one system, approvals in another, incident evidence in tickets, and decisions in hallway conversations. When you need accountability, you’re left stitching together logs across tools and teams.

### 3) What Aura does
Aura is a **voice-native operations and compliance agent** for autonomous factory robotics. It supervises the fleet, summarizes anomalies in an operational format, recommends the next safe step, and requests the **right approval tier (T0–T3)** before any safety- or compliance-relevant action.

### 4) Why voice-native supervision matters
On the floor, operators don’t have time to hunt through dashboards. Voice lets you ask “why is that robot paused?” and get a tight answer—what happened, where, impact, confidence, and what approval is required—without breaking flow.

### 5) Why immutable compliance logging matters
When you approve a cloud inference job, resume after a safety stop, or initiate a safety hold, that decision needs a tamper-evident trail. Aura anchors key events as **immutable compliance records**, with selected decisions **logged on-chain** for auditability.

### 6) Human-in-the-loop governance
Our philosophy is simple: **AI assists. Humans decide.** Aura can recommend and prepare actions, but it won’t cross a safety or compliance boundary without explicit, auditable human confirmation.

### 7) Final one-line takeaway
Aura makes robot operations **deployable and accountable**: voice-first supervision, tiered approvals, and immutable compliance logging—built for real factories.
